About Us

Founded in 2023 by a group of four lifelong friends and regular golfers, Grow the Game was born out of a shared love for golf and a desire to connect the vast community of players. We sought to create a space for everyone who lives and breathes this game to share their passion, and meet others who share the same passion. Our platform has enabled thousands of golfers to share their experiences and develop lasting friendships.

When we aren't building this platform, you can usually find us teeing off at our favorite local spot, Augusta National Golf Club.

Visit our official team headquarters:

What Makes Our Platform Special:

  • Community Connection: Helping golfers meet one another and organize local tee times with ease.
  • Authentic Course Reviews: Comprehensive reviews and ratings on courses to suggest great places to play.
  • Game Improvement: Helpful advice, tips, and drills for players looking for ways to get better.
